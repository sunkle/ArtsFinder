Timed Auction: Happening Now

The Basement Auction

Ends From: Thu 26 November at 10:00
Register To Bid
More Info
All lots are available to view in our Montgomery Street saleroom from Monday to Friday (9am - 5pm).

Each lot ends in chronological order in 30 second intervals, from 10am on Thursday the 26th of November.

If somebody bids on a lot in the final 30 seconds, then the time will be automatically extended up to 30 seconds to ensure that everyone has a chance to respond with a bid.

Due to the tight turnaround nature and the space restrictions attached to our Weekly Basement Auction, all items must be paid for and collected/delivery arranged by 5pm on Friday.

We offer the packaging and fully insured delivery to anywhere in the World.

Any queries in regards to the sale, please contact us on 028 9032 5448.
