Gold, Silver and Platinum Jewellery
At Bloomfield Auctions we have become known as one of the leading auction houses for the sale of Jewellery & Watches. For over 40 years now, we have dealt with Estates of Jewellery and high-end Watch Collections. Our varied specialists are on hand to assist to authenticate items as and when required. We auction Jewellery including gold, platinum or silver jewellery, diamond, ruby, emerald, sapphire and other precious stones. While also stocking watches from brands such as Rotary to Rolex.

Current Auctions

Bloomfield Auctions host a weekly auction every Tuesday commencing at 1pm. Each auction is live in house and via several auction platforms. Each auction offers an eclectic mix of items with something for almost all tastes. Each can be viewed inhouse or online unless otherwise specified. Our viewing days are Mondays 10.00am-6.00pm & Tuesdays 10.00am-12.45pm. Times can vary subject to public/bank holidays, but this will always be notified here on our website.

How to Buy

Buying at auction can be daunting for some people if it is your first time but at Bloomfield Auctions, we aim to make that process as user friendly as possible. By clicking on the link How To Buy we can take you through that process of buying step by step and should you still have any further questions, our staff are always on hand to assist you with any queries you may have.

How to Sell

On behalf of our vendors, we sell single items to complete house clearances to a single owner collection sale. The advice we always give to people, please do not throw anything out, let us see the items or items first because sometimes you do not see as value what we do and vice versa. For some people fees for selling can be unclear which is why we outline our fees at the outset of every appointment to act on your behalf. By clicking on the link How To Sell, we will guide you through the process step by step, however if you remain unsure of anything please contact us as our staff are always on hand to assist you with any queries you may have.
