Modern Art

Thursday 03 December 2026 11:00
Venue: Online & Live Broadcast from Duggleby Stephenson of York

Bold ideas, distinctive perspectives and influential names in British and international art take centre stage in our Modern Art Sale. Spanning painting, printmaking and sculpture, the auction celebrates artistic expression across the 20th century and beyond.

Leading the selection is a signed David Hockney etching and aquatint from Homage to Picasso, alongside Walter Sickert’s Les Deux Tourelles, a figure study by Sir William Orpen, Julian Trevelyan’s Low Tide and a large abstract work by Jan Voss.

With an exciting variety of artistic styles, subjects and techniques represented, this specialist auction presents opportunities for established collectors and those looking to discover something exceptional. Browse the catalogue and bid now.

Viewings

Viewings will be held at Duggleby Stephenson of York, Murton, YO19 5GF

Tuesday 1st December 9am - 4:30pm
Wednesday 2nd December 9am - 4:30pm
Thursday 3rd December from 9am


Buyer's premium 25.00% plus VAT.

In-house post & packing available - see individual lots for prices.

Items to be paid for Thursday, 10 December and collected by Thursday, 10 December
