How to Buy

Browse our auctions, register to bid and bid online, in person, by telephone or by leaving a commission bid. Below you’ll find everything you need to know about fees, bidding, payment and collection.

3. Before you bid

Viewing

Viewing is available by appointment only. If there is an item you would like to inspect before bidding, please contact us.

Contact Us

Condition Reports & Additional Images

Potential bidders are strongly encouraged to gather as much information as possible about a lot before bidding. Hansons offers a complimentary condition report and additional image service by telephone and email.

Condition reports reflect the opinion of Hansons Auctioneers and are not statements of fact. They may not detail issues consistent with an item’s age. Buyers should study all available images and information carefully.

4. Won an item? What happens next?

Receive your invoice

After the auction, successful bidders receive an invoice by email.

Pay

Use the secure payment link in your invoice to pay by credit card, debit card or bank transfer. Telephone payments are accepted up to £500. Cash is not accepted.

Collect or arrange delivery

Contact the relevant saleroom to book a collection appointment, or use the postage and collection service where available.

Collection reminders

If a courier, family member or friend is collecting on behalf of the buyer, Hansons requires the buyer’s authority before the goods are released.

Buyers and couriers should check purchases before leaving the auction house. Hansons will not accept claims for loss or damage after departure.

Timely collection is important as storage space is limited.

5. Important information

Artist’s Resale Right (ARR)

If you buy a work of art by a living artist, or an artist who has died within the last 70 years, which costs more than £1,000, an Artist’s Resale Right royalty may be payable.

Current royalty rates shown on this page:

  • Up to £50,000 — 4%
  • £50,000.01 to £200,000 — 3%
  • £200,000.01 to £350,000 — 1%
  • £350,000.01 to £500,000 — 0.5%
  • Exceeding £500,000 — 0.25%
VAT & Customs Charges

VAT under the margin scheme may be payable and Hansons is unable to refund the same for overseas buyers. Additional customs taxes may also be payable by a buyer or potential seller.

Hansons is not responsible for additional customs taxes. Anyone sending items from overseas to Hansons for valuation or sale may incur customs charges, and customs charges may also apply where lots are dispatched overseas.

Post-Sale Offers

Where an item remains unsold on auction day, Hansons may, acting as agent for the vendor, accept a reasonable offer made during the week following the sale. Such sales incur the same charges as if the lot had sold at auction.

Collection & Storage Terms

On the fall of the hammer, the object becomes the purchaser’s responsibility. Items are insured for up to one week after the sale while on Hansons’ premises. After one week, Hansons accepts no responsibility for damage or loss and buyers should ensure their own insurance covers this.

Paid items not collected within one month may be re-sold on behalf of the buyer, subject to standard terms. Furniture item(s) must be paid for and collected (or an appointment made) within 3 working days after the furniture sale.

Anti-Money Laundering

Hansons Auctioneers operates an anti-money laundering programme. Staff are trained to identify unusual transactions and to take necessary steps to check and verify clients. Buyers or sellers may be asked to provide two forms of identification in order for Hansons to comply with applicable Money Laundering Regulations.

Timed Auctions

Timed auctions are online-only auctions without a live auctioneer. Depending on the individual auction, timed sales are hosted either exclusively on the-saleroom.com, or through Hansons Live and Easy Live Auction. Telephone bidding, room bidding and commission bids placed directly with Hansons are not available for timed auctions.

Timed auction purchases are subject to a buyer’s premium of 28% plus VAT, together with any applicable online bidding charges. Please check the individual auction details to confirm which online bidding platform(s) are available.

Charges and terms can change. Please check the individual auction and lot information before bidding. Full Hansons terms and conditions apply.
