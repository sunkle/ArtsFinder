The Beatles - Sgt. Peppers Lonely Hearts Club Band PCS 7027, a rare 'Nimbus' copy of 1984 Limited Edition UK pressed audiophile remastered pressing on Nimbus/ Parlophone, complete with 'Cut Out' insert. 

NB - This in an incredibly rare and sought after version of the 1967 original release Sgt Peppers Lonely Hearts Club Band. Produced in 1984 by Nimbus Records in a limited run of only 1,000 copies for HIFI magazine promotion. The vendor has had it since 1984 when he acquired it through the promotion.

More Information

Overall VG+
Side One shows a diagonal whispy scratch running across songs 1 - 5.
Side Two has a scratch running from songs 1 - 5.
Both are light and when a finger is run over them can not be felt.
We have not listened to this record and therefore can not comment on the sound quality.
The Cover is very clean with only light wear to the corner and edges.
