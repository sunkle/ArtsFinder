Tools, Machinery & Vehicles

Our sales take place monthly on a Saturday selling from minor hand tools up to heavy plant & machinery. Our popular monthly machinery auction is always well attended and we have great facilities, including a cafe and large car parking area. 

The machinery auction is made up of three areas - the calf ring (minor hand tools), the sheep pens (garden equipment and smaller machines) and the agri yard (farm machinery and implements) - From 2021, the whole auction commences in the cattle ring.

We advise anyone wanting to purchase on the day of sale to come into the office to obtain a buyer's bidding card before the auction begins at 10am.

Bidding on site or online at: MartEye.com

Our next monthly auction of Farm Implements, Tools and Machinery will commence on Saturday, 24th October 2026 at 9.00am
