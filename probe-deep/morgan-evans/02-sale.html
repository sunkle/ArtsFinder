Tools & Machinery Auction

The Morgan Evans & Co Ltd., Tools, Implement and Machinery Sales are held once a month on a Saturday and start at 9.00am. There is usually a vast selection of items for sale from small garden and DIY tools to tractors and trailers.
 

Purchase of goods: no buyers premium is charged. Collection and payment is on the day of sale only.

THERE IS NO GUARANTEE PROVIDED ON ITEMS PURCHASED AT THIS SALE (Please note that VAT is applicable to some items)
