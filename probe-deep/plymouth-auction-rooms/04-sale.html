Selling

Selling at Auction

 Whether you're selling a single item, an inherited collection or the contents of an entire property, Plymouth Auction Rooms offers a straightforward and professional auction service.

Our experienced team will guide you through the process, from initial valuation and advice to cataloguing, marketing and the final sale.

You'll find answers to frequently asked questions below. If you need any further assistance, please get in touch. We're always happy to help.

How do I get my items valued for auction?

How do I get my items valued for auction?

 Plymouth Auction Rooms offers free, no-obligation auction valuations, whether you have a single item or an entire collection.

There are several ways to obtain a valuation:

  1. Visit Our Saleroom

Valuations are available Monday to Thursday, 9am–4pm, with appointments preferred to ensure the appropriate specialist is available.

Simply bring your items along and we'll advise on their likely auction value. If suitable for sale, items can usually be left with us, and we'll provide a receipt and details of the auction.

For larger collections, we recommend sending photographs or contacting us before visiting.

  1. Online Valuations

Visit our Valuations page to upload photographs and details of your items. Our specialists will review them and respond with an initial auction assessment.

  1. Home Visits

For larger collections, house contents and items that are difficult to transport, we offer home valuation visits throughout Devon and Cornwall.

Please contact us or call 01752 254740 to discuss your requirements.

  1. WhatsApp & Video Valuations

Send photographs or a short video directly to us via WhatsApp on 07432 056261 for an initial assessment.

We can also arrange a video consultation where appropriate. Please email info@plymouthauctions.co.uk or call 01752 254740.

 

What about reserves?

A reserve is the confidential minimum price at which an item may be sold.Our specialists will recommend an auction estimate based on current market conditions and, where appropriate, agree a reserve with you before the sale.Please note that we do not accept reserves on lots valued below £100.The auctioneer may exercise discretion to sell a lot up to 10% below the agreed reserve.In certain circumstances, a charge may apply to items that remain unsold due to a higher reserve. Any such charge would be discussed and agreed with you beforehand.

 

How much does it cost to sell at auction?

Selling Commission

Our standard selling commission is 18% plus VAT, calculated on the hammer price.

For Swarovski crystal, our standard commission is 20% plus VAT.

We are happy to discuss individually tailored commission rates for higher-value items and larger collections.

A £5 processing fee per lot also applies. All charges will be explained and agreed before your items are entered for auction.

Insurance

Items in our care are insured against all risks at the midpoint of their auction estimate.

Settlement

Payment to sellers is normally made within 21 days of the auction, by bank transfer or cheque if preferred, provided the buyer has paid in full.

If a buyer fails to pay, we will contact you to discuss the next steps in accordance with our Conditions of Sale.

What if items do not sell?

If a lot does not sell during an auction, there will be a nominal fee at the auctioneer’s discretion. As per these terms, we charge a fee of £10 for premium individual lots and £5 for multiple lots.
