About Philip Serrell Auctioneers

Philip Serrell Auctioneers has been established as one of Worcestershire’s premier independent auction houses since 1995, and experienced in the area for some 40 years.

Whilst the main area of practice covers the whole of Middle England and the Welsh Borders, the reputation of the company now extends nationally and internationally.
