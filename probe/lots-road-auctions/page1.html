For all things authentic, eclectic, iconic and magnificent!

Welcome to Lots Road Auctions – London’s Premier Auction House for Antiques, Art, Design and Luxury Collectibles.

Since 1979, Lots Road Auctions has been a cornerstone of the London auction scene, attracting interior designers, private collectors, and art enthusiasts from around the world.

Whether you’re looking to furnish a home, start a collection, or discover a unique treasure, our auctions are the perfect place to explore a diverse range of carefully curated items. We offer everything from fine art, mid-century modern furniture, and antique decor to contemporary design, vintage lighting, rugs, rare books, luxury goods, and much more.

For over four decades, Lots Road Auctions has earned a reputation for trust, expertise, and passion. Our experienced team of specialists meticulously research and select each item that comes through our doors, ensuring high quality and authenticity across every lot.

Curated. Timeless Design. Sustainable Luxury.

For over four decades, Lots Road has connected buyers, collectors and interior designers with exceptional furniture, fine art, antiques and decorative objects. Every auction is carefully curated by our specialists, bringing together iconic designer pieces, timeless craftsmanship and unique finds that add character and individuality to every home.

Choosing to buy at auction is also a more sustainable way to furnish your space. Investing in pre-owned furniture and works of art helps preserve exceptional design, extends the life of beautifully made pieces and supports a more conscious approach to luxury living. Whether you’re searching for a statement piece, furnishing an entire home or discovering inspiration for your next interior project, our ever-changing auctions offer something truly distinctive.

Explore our specialist departments to discover furniture, lighting, art and decorative objects that celebrate craftsmanship, provenance and enduring style – helping you create interiors with personality and lasting value.

Explore the Lots Road Experience.

We are proud to serve a global community of buyers and sellers. Whether you’re seeking an iconic piece of mid-design, a statement Chesterfield sofa, an original oil painting, or a designer Hermès handbag, you’ll find something to fall in love with at Lots Road.

Our auction house isn’t just a marketplace — it’s a destination for art and design lovers, where heritage meets contemporary style and creativity thrives.

We understand that everyone has a different schedule, so we’ve made it easy to participate in our events, wherever you are. Our live in-room auctions take place regularly throughout each month and offer an exciting way to bid in real-time, either in person, by phone, or online. You can bid with us directly at Lots Road, on The Saleroom, Drouot and Easy Live Auction.

We are open daily between 10am – 5pm and welcome visitors looking to explore the showroom or drop in for a valuation. Whether you’re buying, selling, or just browsing, you’ll find a warm welcome at Lots Road Auctions.

Join our mailing list or follow us on social media to stay up to date with upcoming auctions, featured lots, and life at Lots Road.
